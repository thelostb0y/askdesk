# Engineering Onboarding Guide

New engineers receive laptop, repo access, and a staging environment on day
one. The onboarding buddy is assigned by the team lead before the start date.

## First week

Days 1-2 are environment setup and the architecture walkthrough. Days 3-5 are
a starter ticket: a small, production-bound change shipped with a buddy
review. Every new engineer ships to production in their first week.

## Access

Production database access is granted only after the security training is
completed, and is read-only by default. Write access requires a documented
incident or migration task approved by the on-call lead.
